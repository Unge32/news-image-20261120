MARKETS — LEAD STORY
STOCKS SLIDE AGAIN AS THE AI BOOM WOBBLES
The S&P 500 falls 2.0% for a fifth straight loss, the Dow drops 2.2% and Japan’s Nikkei sinks 4.7% — and not even Anthropic’s $2 trillion Nasdaq debut can lift the market, as investors fret over AI valuations, high rates and the Gulf crisis.
DIPLOMACY — BERLIN
Europe ready to talk to Russia on ending the war
Ukraine’s European allies meet in Berlin and say they have a united position for negotiations with Russia. Moscow answers by inviting European leaders to talks — a day after Trump, Xi and Putin issued their four-point peace declaration in Shenzhen.
CLIMATE — ANTALYA
COP31 draft drops fossil-fuel phase-out
Negotiators at the COP31 climate summit present a second draft that removes the call to phase out fossil fuels — the words “oil” and “fossil fuels” no longer appear at all. The final declaration is expected today, Friday.
WAR — KYIV
Four ballistic missiles hit Kyiv
Kyiv is hit by four ballistic missiles in the early hours, and a tanker catches fire after striking a mine east of the Strait of Hormuz as Iran widens its attacks on Gulf shipping.
TECH — CALIFORNIA
California subpoenas Nvidia over China chips
California’s attorney general opens a probe into whether Nvidia’s advanced chips reached China, issuing a subpoena days after the ouster of chief executive Jensen Huang amid a suspected spying scandal.
SPORT — WASHINGTON
Trump pardons Pete Rose, baseball’s hit king
President Trump gives a posthumous pardon to Pete Rose, the all-time hits leader, who died in 2024 aged 83 after a lifetime ban for betting on games. Rose was reinstated to baseball last year.
